# Sri Vasavi Engineering College (SVEC) — Attendance Regulations & Policies

## 1. Minimum Attendance Requirement
- Every student admitted to B.Tech, M.Tech, or MBA programmes at Sri Vasavi Engineering College (Autonomous), Tadepalligudem, must maintain a minimum aggregate attendance of 75% in all registered subjects and laboratory practicals during each semester.
- Regular class attendance is vital for academic progress, continuous evaluation, and eligibility for Semester End Examinations (SEE).

## 2. Condonation of Attendance Shortage (Medical & Legitimate Grounds)
- Condonation of shortage of attendance between 65% and 74.9% may be granted by the College Academic Committee (CAC), chaired by the Principal.
- Eligibility for condonation is strictly reserved for genuine medical reasons, participation in national/state-level sports competitions, NCC, NSS camps, or approved college technical events.
- Condonation Procedure:
  1. The student or guardian must submit a formal application along with a valid medical certificate issued by a registered medical practitioner (Civil Surgeon / Authorized Medical Officer) within 3 working days of returning to campus.
  2. The respective Head of Department (HOD) reviews and forwards the application to the Principal.
  3. If approved, a prescribed condonation fee must be paid before the deadline announced by the Examination Section.
- Condonation cannot be claimed as a matter of right.

## 3. Detention Due to Attendance Shortage
- Any student who secures less than 65% aggregate attendance in a semester cannot seek condonation under any circumstances.
- Such students are declared DETAINED for want of attendance.
- Consequences of Detention:
  1. Detained students are not permitted to write the Semester End Examinations (SEE) for that semester.
  2. The student must register again for the entire semester course work when it is offered in the subsequent academic year, subject to academic regulations and fee guidelines.
  3. All internal marks for that semester become void upon detention.

## 4. Attendance Monitoring & Communication
- Attendance is recorded on an hourly basis and uploaded to the college ERP portal daily.
- SMS alerts and automated notifications are sent to parents/guardians at the end of every month.
- Monthly attendance percentages are displayed on departmental notice boards.
- Students with attendance dropping below 75% are summoned for counselling by their designated faculty mentors and Head of Department.
