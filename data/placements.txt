# Sri Vasavi Engineering College (SVEC) — Training & Placement Cell Overview

## 1. Training & Placement Cell (TAP Cell) Mission
- The Training and Placement Cell at Sri Vasavi Engineering College, Tadepalligudem, actively facilitates campus recruitment drives, industry-readiness training, internships, and career counselling.
- SVEC maintains strong relationships with IT multinationals, core engineering enterprises, startup incubators, and national recruiting partners.
- More than 80+ recruiting companies visit the SVEC campus or conduct pool campus drives annually.

## 2. Salary Packages and Placement Highlights
- Highest Salary Package: Approximately Rs 13 Lakhs Per Annum (LPA) in recent placement drives.
- Average Salary Package: Ranges between Rs 4.0 LPA and 5.5 LPA across IT and engineering branches.
- Total Offers: SVEC students consistently secure 600+ to 800+ placement offers in an academic year.
- Prominent Recruiters:
  - Tata Consultancy Services (TCS) (National Qualifier Test drives, Digital & Ninja profiles)
  - Infosys (Power Programmer & SE roles)
  - Cognizant (GenC, GenC Next, GenC Elevate)
  - Wipro (Elite & Turbo National Talent Hunt)
  - Accenture Solutions
  - L&T Technology Services (LTTS)
  - Capgemini
  - Hexaware Technologies
  - Tech Mahindra
  - Mindtree / LTIMindtree
  - Efftronics Systems (Core Electronics & Embedded Systems)
  - Hyundai Steel & Automotive core recruiters

## 3. Campus Recruitment Training (CRT) & Skill Enhancement
- Pre-placement training commences right from the 2nd and 3rd year of B.Tech.
- Quantitative Aptitude, Logical Reasoning, and Verbal Ability training delivered by reputed corporate training agencies.
- Technical & Coding Bootcamps: Hands-on coding sessions in C, Java, Python, Data Structures & Algorithms (DSA), Web Technologies, and SQL.
- Mock Interviews & Soft Skills: Group discussions, technical mock interviews conducted by alumni and industry specialists, resume critique sessions, and professional communication workshops.
- AICTE IDEA Lab on campus provides 24x7 hardware and prototyping tools for practical design and product development.

## 4. Placement Eligibility Guidelines for Students
- Minimum Academic Criteria: Generally, recruiters demand a minimum aggregate of 60% or 6.5 CGPA throughout 10th, 12th/Diploma, and B.Tech.
- Backlogs: Candidates should ideally have zero active backlogs at the time of appearing for placement selection processes. A few companies permit up to 1 standing backlog.
- Attendance Policy: Students must possess at least 75% attendance in CRT classes to remain eligible for college placement sponsorship.
- Placement Code of Conduct: Once a student receives an offer (Job 1), they may be eligible to compete for a "Dream Offer" or "Super Dream Offer" (higher salary tier usually 1.5x to 2x higher) as specified by the TAP Cell guidelines.
